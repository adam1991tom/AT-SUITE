# Scenario: a stage screen goes blank

**The problem:** the stage timer screen in Main Hall is black, frozen, or showing the wrong thing, and the speaker is on in five minutes.

## 1. Is it Blackout? (5 seconds)

Look at the room timer. If **Blackout** is lit, someone blanked the screens. Press it again. See [Stage messages, blink, clock and blackout](/docs/stage-messages).

## 2. Is it showing the right view? (30 seconds)

Open the **Screens** window in the workspace (or look at **SCREENS** in the top bar: it says how many of the room's screens are online). Each screen has a dot (online or not) and what it shows.

- Showing **nothing yet** or the wrong view: pick the view from the list and press **Show**.
- Not sure which screen is which: **Identify** puts its name on it.

## 3. Is it online? (1 minute)

A hollow dot or **Offline** in the [pre-show check](/docs/preshow-check) means the server hasn't heard from it.

- Check the screen's computer is on and awake, and its network cable is in.
- On the console, **Timers → Screens**: press **Reload** (a browser screen) or **Restart** (a Linux screen). See [Linux screens](/docs/linux-screens).

## 4. Is it the display, not AT SUITE? (1 minute)

If the screen's computer is fine but the display is black: check the display is on the right input, the HDMI or SDI cable, and any switcher or splitter in between. Plug a laptop in directly to test.

## 5. Get a timer on stage another way

While you fix it, give the speaker something:

- Open the timer on any spare laptop: the stage view's link is **Timer views ↗** on the room timer. Make it full screen (F11).
- With the AT SUITE app, pop the timer out onto a second monitor or a confidence screen fed from the laptop.
- Last resort: a **stage message** on the screens that do work, or hold up a sign.

## 6. If you can't fix it

**Help → Call for help**, category **Video**, "Stage screen in Main Hall is black, tried reload". The AI helper starts working through it with you while someone comes.

## Afterwards

Leave a [handover note](/docs/handover-notes) saying what happened and what fixed it, and when the call is resolved, write how it was fixed so the AI helper can offer it next time.
