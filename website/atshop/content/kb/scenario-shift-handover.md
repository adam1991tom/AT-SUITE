# Scenario: shift handover

**The moment:** Amy's shift in Main Hall ends at 13:00 and Ben takes over for the afternoon, while the room keeps running.

## Amy, before she leaves

1. **Handover notes**: write what Ben needs, and tick **Pin to the top** for anything that stays true all day (see [Handover notes](/docs/handover-notes)):
    - what's next and anything unusual ("Panel at 14:00 needs 4 handhelds, they're charged on the rack"),
    - what went wrong and how it was fixed,
    - anything still open ("Projector 2 fan is noisy, venue knows").
2. Leave the timer on the next cue: **Load** it so Ben just presses **GO**.
3. If captions are running, leave them running. Ben takes over the mic on his own laptop when he's ready.

## Ben, when he sits down

1. On the room's laptop: Amy presses **Sign out**, Ben signs in and starts the day in Main Hall. The timer, cue list, captions settings, chat and notes all belong to the room, so nothing is lost.
2. He reads the notes (the **NOTES** count in the top bar), and ticks off the ones he's dealt with.
3. He checks the room's **chat** for anything in the last hour.

## If Ben works on his own laptop

He signs in on his laptop and picks Main Hall as Main PC. If captions should come from his laptop, he presses **Send my mic** there: Amy's mic stops and she's told who took over.

## Main PC and Backup PC

If both laptops stay in the room, one is the **Main PC** (no pop-ups or sounds, nothing on its screen but the show) and the other the **Backup PC**. Swap them from **This laptop** if the main operator changes seats. See [Start the day](/docs/start-the-day).
