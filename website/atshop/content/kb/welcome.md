# Welcome to AT SUITE

AT SUITE runs the technical side of a live event from one place: the room timers and stage screens, crew chat and help calls, live captions, presenters' slides, and every tech laptop and screen in the venue. It replaces a drawer of separate tools (a show timer, a chat app, a caption service, screen test tools and a spreadsheet of laptops) with one system made for show days.

![The tech workspace on a laptop in the Main Hall: the room timer and cue list on the left, quick timers in the middle, chat and handover notes on the right](/static/kb/workspace-main.jpg "The tech workspace: everything a tech needs for their room, in one window.")

## The parts

| Part | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **The server** | One small Linux machine at the venue (or one we host for you). It keeps the rooms, people, chat, timers and settings, and every other part talks to it. | Nobody directly: it just runs |
| **The console** | The web pages for running the venue: the dashboard, chat, help requests, timers, presenters, captions, rooms, people, laptops, settings and reports. Open the server's address in any browser. | Managers and admins |
| **The tech workspace** | The tech's screen for their room: the timer, cue list, stage messages, chat, help, captions, handover notes and links, as windows they can arrange. | Techs |
| **The AT SUITE app** | The Windows app on each tech laptop. It shows the tech workspace, makes captions from the laptop's microphone, keeps the timer going if the network drops, and can put the timer over the laptop's screen. | Techs |
| **The manager view** | A desk of windows for admins and managers: every room's timer, help requests, the pre-show check, laptops and chat. In the app, or at `/manage` on any browser or phone. | Managers and admins |
| **Screens** | Any screen that shows something: the stage timer, the backstage clock, captions for the audience, a test pattern. A Linux screen, a browser, or a video mixer's browser source. | The audience and the stage |

## How a show day goes

1. Each tech opens the AT SUITE app on their laptop, signs in, picks their room and says whether the laptop is the **Main PC** (on the projector, never any pop-ups) or the **Backup PC**. See [Start the day on a tech laptop](/docs/start-the-day).
2. A manager opens the dashboard and checks the [pre-show check](/docs/preshow-check) is green: every laptop, screen and caption mic, room by room.
3. Techs run the room: the [timer and cue list](/docs/run-the-timer), [stage messages](/docs/stage-messages), [captions](/docs/captions-quick-start) and [chat](/docs/chat).
4. When something goes wrong, the tech asks the [AI helper](/docs/ai-helper) or [calls for help](/docs/help-calls), and the crew answers from anywhere in the venue.
5. Between shifts, techs leave [handover notes](/docs/handover-notes) for the next person.
6. After the show, a manager downloads the [show report](/docs/show-reports) for the client.
