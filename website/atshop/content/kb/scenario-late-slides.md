# Scenario: late slides from a presenter

**The problem:** Dr Lee is on at 10:00. At 09:48 she says she's changed a slide.

## If she can upload it herself (fastest)

1. She opens her presenter link on her phone or laptop and presses **Upload a new version**. See [Presenter links](/docs/presenter-links).
2. The room's tech sees **New slides from Dr Hannah Lee** in the bell, and the **Sessions** window shows the new file **Waiting for review**.
3. The tech opens it to check it, then presses **Approve** in **Sessions**.
4. [Room sync](/docs/room-sync) puts it on the presentation laptop within about 20 seconds, replacing the old version in the session's folder.

## If she has it on a USB stick

1. The tech (or the desk) opens **Presenters → Running order**, opens the session, and presses **Upload for them** on her line.
2. Approve it. Room sync takes it from there.

Copying it straight onto the presentation laptop also works in a hurry, but upload it too afterwards, so the record (and the next day's run) has the right version.

## Check before she walks on

- Open the file on the presentation laptop. Check the changed slide, any video, and that it's 16:9.
- Press **Check in** for her in **Sessions** if she hasn't pressed **I'm here**.

## If it's wrong

**Reject** it with a note ("The video doesn't play, please send the MP4 separately"). She sees the note on her page straight away.
