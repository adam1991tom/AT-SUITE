# Presenter links, uploads and check-in

Every presenter has a private link to their own page. There's no account and no password: the link is the key, so send it only to them.

![A presenter's page on a phone: Future of Events Summit, Hello Dr Hannah Lee, the keynote's day, time and room, the slides marked Received, the AV team will check it, an Upload a new version box, and Checked in under On the day](/static/kb/presenter-portal.jpg "What a presenter sees on their link.")

## Send the link

On **Presenters → Running order**, open the session. Each presenter has their link in a box with **Copy link**. Paste it into your email to them. The page uses your venue's logo and colour.

## What the presenter does

1. Opens the link on any phone or computer. It shows their session, its time and room, and your **note on every presenter's page**.
2. **Upload your slides**: drag the file onto the box, or tap to choose it. A bar shows the upload. It can be up to the size limit you set.
3. Their page then says where it is:
    - **Received, the AV team will check it** (waiting for review),
    - **Approved, ready in the room**,
    - **Please send a new version**, with your note.
4. To change their slides, they upload again. Each upload is a new version (v1, v2...), and the newest is the one that counts.
5. On the day, they press **I'm here** when they arrive. The room's tech sees them as **here** straight away.

## What you can do for them

On their line in the running order:

| Button | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Upload for them** | They've emailed the file or handed you a USB stick |
| **Check in** / **Undo check-in** | They've arrived at the desk, or you pressed it by mistake |
| **Edit** | Fix their name, email or phone |
| **New link** | The link went to the wrong person. The old link stops working at once |
| **Remove** | Takes them and their files off the event |

## Questions presenters ask

**"The link says it isn't valid."** The event may be archived, or someone made a new link for them. Send the current one.

**"It won't upload."** Check it's under the size limit (the page says the limit). On a slow connection, keep the page open until the bar finishes. Or take the file on a stick and use **Upload for them**.

**"Did you get my slides?"** Their page shows each version they sent, with when.

> **Note:** Presenter pages show nothing about other presenters, rooms or the venue. Archiving the event switches every link off.
