# Scenario: the licence is ending

**What you see:** a banner across the console: your licence ends soon, or the server couldn't check in.

## First: it never stops a show

AT SUITE never locks during a show. If a lock is due, it waits for a restart, or for 02:00 to 05:00 venue time with no timer running and no captions live. So finish the show, then sort it out.

## "Your licence ends on..."

| When | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days before (7 for keys that renew themselves) | Admins | Check your subscription is active in [your account](/account) |
| 7 days before | Everyone, as a small line | Renew, or tell your admin |
| After it ends | Everyone | 14 days of grace: everything still works. Renew now |
| After the grace | Everyone | AT SUITE locks. Only **Licence** works. Nothing is deleted |

When your subscription renews, the server fetches the new key by itself at its next daily check-in. To do it now: **Licence → Check now**.

If renewal didn't happen (an expired card, say), update the payment method in your account: see [Billing and your account](/docs/billing). Then **Check now**.

## "Couldn't check in"

The server sends its key to the AT SUITE website once a day. If it can't:

- Check the server can reach the internet (a firewall, a proxy, a venue network change).
- **Licence → Check now** tries straight away.
- Admins see a note after 2 days, everyone in the last week. After 14 days without a check-in it locks (never during a show).

A venue that can never reach the internet should use an **offline key**: [contact us](/contact).

## Paste a key in by hand

Your new key is in [your account](/account) and in the renewal email. **Licence → Install a new key**, paste it, **Install licence**. It takes effect at once, and a locked server unlocks straight away. See [Licence keys](/docs/licence-keys).

## More laptops than your plan

The licence page shows how many laptops you're using and your limit. Remove old laptops on **Laptops & screens**, or move to a bigger plan in your account and paste the new key.
