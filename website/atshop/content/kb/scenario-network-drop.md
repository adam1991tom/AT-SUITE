# Scenario: the network drops mid-show

**The moment:** 10:12, the keynote is running, and the top bar's **live** label turns to *connecting*. The server can't be reached.

## What keeps working, with the AT SUITE app

- **The timer**: every button still works on your laptop. A bar at the bottom says **the timer is running on this laptop**. Your workspace and the AT Overlay show it.
- **Captions**: your laptop keeps turning speech into text. What's said meanwhile is sent when the server's back, so the transcript has no gap.
- **Screens fed from your laptop**: if you ticked **Feed this room's screens from this laptop**, the caption and timer screens pointed at your laptop keep going.

What waits: chat, help calls, handover notes, and screens that get the timer from the server. Full detail: [When the network drops](/docs/when-the-network-drops).

## The first minute

1. **Don't stop the timer.** Keep pressing what you'd normally press. Everything you do is remembered.
2. **Look at the stage screen.** If it froze, the speaker can't see time. Pop the timer out onto a confidence monitor from your laptop, or tick **Feed this room's screens from this laptop** and point a screen at the address it shows.
3. **Tell the crew** the old-fashioned way: radio or comms. Chat is waiting too.
4. **Note the time** it went down, for the report.

## While it's down

- Is it the room or the venue? If other rooms' laptops still say **live**, it's your laptop or its cable. Check the network cable or Wi-Fi.
- If the whole venue is down, it's the network or the server: whoever looks after them needs to know. AT SUITE can't fix a dead switch.
- Captions: leave **Send my mic** on.

## When it comes back

- The **live** label comes back. Everything you pressed goes to every screen as of when you pressed it: a cue started five minutes ago shows five minutes gone.
- If someone changed the room's timer at the server while you were cut off, the latest change wins, and you're told.
- Captions said meanwhile appear on the server, and the note says so.
- Chat and help calls sent from other places arrive.

## Afterwards

- Leave a [handover note](/docs/handover-notes) with what happened and when.
- If it's the server that went, check **Settings → About** and the pre-show check for anything still red.

> **Tip:** Prepare for this before the show: tick **Feed this room's screens from this laptop** on the Main PC of your most important room, and know where you'd plug in a confidence monitor.
