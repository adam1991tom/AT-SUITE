# Ask the AI helper

The AI helper is a chat in the tech workspace for when something isn't working, or you don't know how to do something in AT SUITE. It asks questions to narrow a fault down, and it can call for help for you.

![The AI helper window: a tech says the lectern mic is dead, the helper asks whether the channel on the desk shows signal, with quick answer buttons below, and This fixed it and Call for help at the bottom](/static/kb/workspace-ai.jpg "The AI helper window in the workspace.")

## Ask it something

1. Open **Help** in the top bar. Under **🤖 AI helper**, type what's wrong, or what you want to do, in plain words, and press **Ask**.
2. The **AI helper** window opens with its answer. Keep talking: answer its questions, or tap one of the quick answers under the chat.
3. Each answer says where it came from under **From:**, a venue note, a past fix, the tech guide or the AT SUITE manual. Click it to read the whole thing.
4. **New chat** starts again.

You can open the window any time from **Layout → AI helper**, too.

## What it knows

It answers from, in this order:

1. **Your venue's notes**: what your admins wrote about your rooms and kit ("Hall A projector: use input HDMI 2").
2. **How past help calls were fixed** at your venue, and answers techs marked **This fixed it**.
3. **The built-in tech guide**: fault finding for sound, video, lighting, staging, networks, presenters' laptops, hybrid events, safety and running the show.
4. **The AT SUITE manual**: these help pages, so it can answer "how do I..." questions about AT SUITE itself, step by step.

With a private AI model on your network (see [Set up the AI helper](/docs/ai-helper-setup)) it writes its replies from all of that and the whole conversation. Without one, it still chats: it offers the best match and asks the guide's questions to narrow things down. Either way, nothing goes to the internet unless your admin turned on web search.

## When it's fixed

Press **This fixed it**. The helper keeps that answer for your first question, so the next tech who asks something similar gets it first. Then type anything to start a new chat.

## When it isn't

Press **Call for help** in the AI helper window. It sends an urgent help call for your room with what you've told it, and the crew sees it straight away (see [Help calls](/docs/help-calls)). Keep chatting with the helper while they're on the way.

When you send a help call from the Help menu, the AI helper picks it up too and starts helping while someone comes.

## Good questions

- Say what you see, not what you think is wrong: what's on the screen, what the meter does, what the light on the kit shows.
- Name the room's kit if you know it: the make of the radio mic, which projector.
- For AT SUITE itself, ask the way you'd ask a colleague: "how do I add a stage screen?", "how do I export the transcript?", "where do I change the warning time?".

> **Safety:** For anything involving rigging, power or a person's safety, stop and call your supervisor. The AI helper's safety answers are a reminder, not a replacement for your venue's procedures.
