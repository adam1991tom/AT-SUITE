# Who can do what

Everyone who uses AT SUITE has an account with one of four roles. Admins and managers add accounts under **People** (see [People, roles and passwords](/docs/people-and-passwords)).

| Role | Can | Can't |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything: all the Live and Venue pages, plus **Settings**, **Licence**, **Backups**, **Bug reports** and adding other admins. | |
| **Manager** | Run the venue: the Live pages (dashboard, chat, help requests, timers, presenters, captions, show reports), the Venue pages (rooms, laptops & screens, people, links) and read the audit log. Open the manager view. | Change settings, the licence, backups or API keys; see the enrolment code that adds laptops; make admins. |
| **Tech** | Run their room from the tech workspace: the timer and cue list, stage messages, chat, help calls, captions, handover notes, screens and overlays. | Open the console's Venue and Site pages. |
| **Viewer** | Read: see the timers and chat. | Change anything. |

A manager only sees the pages they can use: a page that isn't theirs isn't in their side bar.

![The console side bar for a manager: Live and Venue pages, with no Site section](/static/kb/console-manager-sidebar.jpg "What a manager sees: no Settings, Licence or Backups.")

## Limiting someone to one venue

If your licence covers more than one venue (site), an account can be limited to one of them under **People → Site**. They only see that venue's rooms, chat and laptops.

## Main PC and Backup PC

Each tech laptop is a **Main PC** or a **Backup PC** for the day. This isn't a permission; it decides what pops up on that laptop:

- **Main PC**: the laptop on the projector or the stage feed. Nothing ever pops up on it, so nothing can appear in front of the audience.
- **Backup PC**: chat for the tech's room, crew-wide and urgent messages, and help calls pop up bottom-right, on top of everything including full-screen slides. They never make a sound and never take the keyboard.

The tech picks one when they start the day, and can change it in **This laptop**. See [Notifications, pop-ups and the bell](/docs/notifications).

## Admins and managers on a tech laptop

An admin or manager who signs in on a tech laptop gets the [manager view](/docs/manager-view) instead of a room. To work a room like a tech, they press **Work a room** in the manager view and start the day as a tech.
