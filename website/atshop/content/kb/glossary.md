# Words we use

**Admin**: someone who can change everything, including settings, the licence and backups. See [Who can do what](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

**AI helper**: a chat in the tech workspace that helps find and fix faults and answers questions about AT SUITE. See [Ask the AI helper](/docs/ai-helper).

**AT Overlay**: the room timer shown in a see-through window over a tech laptop's screen. Clicks go through it. See [Overlays](/docs/overlays).

**AT LiveOverlay**: a separate Windows app for graphics overlays; AT SUITE can turn its overlays on and off. See [Overlays](/docs/overlays).

**Backstage view**: a screen view with a studio clock, the timer, the running order and help calls, for the stage manager. See [Timer views](/docs/timer-views).

**Backup PC**: a tech laptop that shows silent pop-ups for chat and help calls. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).

**Blackout**: blanks the room's stage screens until it's pressed again.

**Blink**: makes the timer flash once time is up, or a stage message flash while it shows.

**Console**: the web pages admins and managers use to run the venue. See [The console at a glance](/docs/console-tour).

**Cue**: one item in the running order, usually a session, with a title and a length. See [Build the running order](/docs/cue-list).

**Cue list**: the room's running order: every cue, in order.

**End action**: what a cue does when its time runs out: keep going into overtime, stop, load the next cue, or play the next.

**Enrolment code**: the code a laptop or screen uses to join the venue the first time. It's at the top of **Laptops & screens → Add laptops**.

**Handover notes**: notes a tech leaves for the next person in their room. See [Handover notes](/docs/handover-notes).

**Help call**: a request for help from a room, which the crew sees straight away. See [Call for help](/docs/help-calls).

**Layout**: how the windows in the workspace are arranged. You can save layouts by name.

**Layout designer**: where admins make their own timer views. See [The layout designer](/docs/layout-designer).

**Main PC**: the tech laptop on the projector or stage feed. Nothing ever pops up on it.

**Manager**: someone who runs the venue day to day but can't change settings or the licence.

**Manager view**: the admins' and managers' desk of windows. See [The manager view](/docs/manager-view).

**Node**: any laptop or screen joined to AT SUITE. You'll see it in older pages and the API.

**Pre-show check**: the dashboard's room-by-room check of every laptop, screen and caption mic. See [The pre-show check](/docs/preshow-check).

**Quick message**: a ready-made stage message techs tap, like "5 minutes" or "Please wrap up".

**Quick timer**: a one-tap countdown (3 to 60 minutes) that doesn't need a cue.

**Room**: a space with its own timer, chat, captions and laptops, like Main Hall or Studio 1.

**Second line**: a line of text under the timer on the stage screens, like "Q&A next".

**Show day**: 05:00 to 05:00 the next morning, venue time. Laptops reset and show reports are cut at 05:00.

**Site**: a venue. Most servers have one; a licence can allow more.

**Speaker preview**: a screen view with the studio clock, every help call on the site and important crew messages, without the stage timer.

**Stage message**: text that shows on the room's stage screens in place of the timer.

**Tech guide**: the AI helper's built-in show-day fault finding (sound, video, lighting, networks and more). Admins can change it.

**Timer view**: how a stage screen shows the timer: Standard, Minimal, Clock, Backstage, BDNG, or one you made.

**Vocabulary**: names and words added to a room so captions spell them right.
