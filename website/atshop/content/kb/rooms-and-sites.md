# Rooms and venues (sites)

A **room** is a space with its own timer, chat, captions, laptops and screens. A **site** is a venue: a building with its own rooms, chat and enrolment code. Most servers have one site.

Open **Rooms** in the console (admins and managers).

![The Rooms page: the Riverside Centre site with its time zone, and four rooms, each with an order number, name, short name and an On tick box](/static/kb/console-rooms.jpg)

## Change a room

Each row has:

- **Order**: rooms are listed lowest first everywhere (the workspace, the dashboard, the start screen).
- **Room**: its full name, like *Main Hall*.
- **Short name**: up to 12 characters, used where space is tight.
- **On**: untick to take a room out of use without deleting it.

Press **Save** on the row. A renamed room keeps its chat, timer, laptops and screens; its chat channel takes the new name.

## Add a room

Type the name in **New room name** at the bottom and press **Add room**. It appears on every laptop's start screen straight away.

## Delete a room

Press **Delete** on the row. Its timer, cues and room chat go with it, so if you might need it again, untick **On** instead.

## The site's name and time zone

The top of the page has the site's name and its time zone. Change them and press **Save**. The time zone decides the venue time in the top bar, when the show day starts (05:00), and when the nightly backup runs.

## Add a second venue

If your licence allows more than one site, type a name in **New site (venue) name** and press **Add site**. The new site gets its own rooms, its own crew chat and its own enrolment code (in **Laptops & screens → Add laptops**). You can limit people to one site under **People**.
