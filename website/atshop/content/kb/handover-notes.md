# Handover notes

Each room has notes that one tech leaves for the next: "clicker 2 needs batteries", "the client wants the house lights at 50% for Q&A". A note stays until someone presses **Done**, so it carries over between shifts and days.

![The Handover notes window: a box to write a note with Pin to the top and Post note, then a pinned note from Mia Patel about house lights, with Unpin, Edit and Done](/static/kb/workspace-notes.jpg)

## Leave a note

1. Open **Handover notes** (it's a tab next to Captions and Add a screen, or click **NOTES** in the top bar).
2. Type the note in the box.
3. Tick **Pin to the top** for standing information about the room that everyone should see first.
4. Press **Post note**.

## Read and tick off notes

- Notes are cards with the author, their initials and how long ago it was written.
- Notes you haven't read yet are counted on the window's tab and on **NOTES** in the top bar. Nothing pops up, on the Main PC or anywhere.
- **Done** ticks a note off. It stays greyed out for a week, with **Put back**, then it's deleted after 30 days.
- **Pin** or **Unpin**, and **Edit**, on any note.

## Good to know

- A tech laptop only sees its own room's notes. Managers see every room's.
- Notes are stored encrypted on the server.
