# Scenario: give the client a view of the show

**The ask:** the client's producer wants to see every room's timer and what's running, on their own laptop or phone, without being able to change anything.

## 1. A viewer account (2 minutes)

**Console → People**: add them with the role **viewer** and the right site. Viewers can see but not change: no timer buttons, no settings. See [Who can do what](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

Give them the username and let them sign in with `ChangeMe`, so they choose their own password.

## 2. What they open

- **The console Dashboard**: every room's timer live, what's on now and next.
- **A room's timer view** in its own browser tab, no sign-in needed: the room's **Standard ↗** link on the Dashboard, or another view such as **Speaker preview** (`/timer/<room>?view=preview`). Send them the link. See [Timer views](/docs/timer-views).
- On a phone, the Dashboard fits the screen.

## 3. A screen for the client's table

For a screen at the client's desk, pair a screen to the room like any other (see [Add a stage screen](/docs/pair-a-screen)) and give it the **Speaker preview** or **Minimal** view.

## 4. Afterwards

Send them the [show report](/docs/show-reports): every session's timing, what ran over, help calls and stage messages, as a PDF. Untick **Active** on their account on **People** when the event is over.

> **Tip:** Keep crew chat out of what clients see. Viewers can read chat, so if the crew needs to be frank, use direct messages, or don't tick **Include the crew's chat messages** on the report.
