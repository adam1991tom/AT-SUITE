# Find your way around the workspace

Everything a tech uses is a window in the workspace, like on Windows: the timer, quick timers, messages, cue list, chat, captions, handover notes and the rest. Arrange them however suits the room, and AT SUITE remembers it on that laptop.

![The workspace: the top bar across the top; the room timer and cue list on the left; quick timers, messages and second line as tabs in the middle; chat, captions, Add a screen and handover notes on the right](/static/kb/workspace-main.jpg)

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **Who and where**: your name, the laptop, **Main PC** or **Backup PC**, and the room.
- **Layout**: every window, and your saved layouts (below). A number on it counts windows with something unread.
- **Live info**: the venue time, the room's timer (in its warning and danger colours, with "message on stage" or "blackout" when they're on), the session now or next with how many presenters are here, how many of the room's **screens** are connected, whether captions are live, the **notes** count, and the venue weather. Click any of them to open its window.
- **live** (or *connecting*): whether the laptop can reach the server.
- **The bell** (Backup PC and browsers): what's new. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).
- **Help**: call for help, ask the AI helper, open the manual or report a bug.
- **This laptop** (in the app), **Admin** and **Sign out**.

## The windows

| Window | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Room timer | The countdown, GO, start or pause, stop, more or less time, blink, blackout and a stage message. See [Run the room timer](/docs/run-the-timer) |
| Quick timers | One-tap countdowns from 3 to 60 minutes, and the clock |
| Messages | Ready-made stage messages. See [Stage messages](/docs/stage-messages) |
| Second line | A line of text under the timer on the stage screens |
| Cue list | The running order. See [Build the running order](/docs/cue-list) |
| Chat | Your room, all crew and direct messages. See [Chat with the crew](/docs/chat) |
| Captions and Captions controls | What's being captioned, and the mic. See [Caption a room](/docs/captions-quick-start) |
| Add a screen | Pair a stage screen by its code. See [Add a stage screen](/docs/pair-a-screen) |
| Screens and Screens preview | What the room's screens show, and a live look at one |
| Now and next, Sessions | Today's presenters' sessions. See [Presenter links and files](/docs/presenter-links) |
| Handover notes | Notes for the next tech. See [Handover notes](/docs/handover-notes) |
| Overlays | AT Overlay and AT LiveOverlay. See [Overlays](/docs/overlays) |
| AI helper | Ask what's wrong. See [Ask the AI helper](/docs/ai-helper) |
| Links | The room's shortcuts. See [Links](/docs/links) |

Some windows only appear when there's something for them: Sessions when there's a presenters event today, Overlays when the room has an overlay laptop.

## Move and arrange windows

- **Dock**: drag a window by its title. Guides appear: drop it beside another window, in its middle to add it as a **tab**, or along an edge of the screen.
- **Float**: let go anywhere else and it floats on top. Floating windows resize from any edge.
- **Resize**: drag the bars between docked windows. With the keyboard, focus a bar and use the arrow keys; double-click a bar to share the space evenly.
- **Window buttons** (top right of each window): **⧉** pop out, **–** minimise, **□** maximise (or double-click the title), **×** close.
- **Pop out** puts a window in a window of its own, for a second monitor. It keeps running exactly as it was. In the AT SUITE app it can stay on top of everything; closing it puts it back.

## The Layout menu

![The Layout menu open: every window with whether it's open, behind another tab or closed, then saved layouts](/static/kb/workspace-layout-menu.jpg)

- Click a window to open it, bring it forward or minimise it.
- **Save this layout** keeps the arrangement under a name (like *Plenary* or *Captions only*) to switch back to with one click.
- **Reset to the starting layout** puts everything back where it started. Use it if a window has gone missing.
- An admin signed in to the workspace can choose **Use on every laptop**, to make their arrangement the starting layout for every tech. Techs can still change their own.
