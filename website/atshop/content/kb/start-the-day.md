# Start the day on a tech laptop

Each morning the tech laptop starts fresh, so the next tech signs in as themselves. It takes ten seconds.

1. Open the **AT SUITE** app (it starts with Windows).
2. Type your **username** and **password**. First time, or after a reset? You'll be asked to choose your own password.
3. Check the **room**. Today's room for this laptop is already picked; pick another if you've moved.
4. Choose what **this PC** is:
    - **Main PC**: the laptop on the projector or stage feed. No pop-ups or notifications at all.
    - **Backup PC**: pop-ups on top for chat and help calls. Never any sound.
5. Press **Start**.

![The start screen on the laptop STUDIO-2: username, password, Forgot password, the four rooms with Studio 2 picked, and Main PC or Backup PC](/static/kb/workspace-start.jpg)

The workspace opens with your room's timer, cue list, chat and the rest. See [Find your way around the workspace](/docs/workspace-tour).

> **Note:** If your venue hasn't turned on tech passwords, the start screen asks for your name instead of a username and password.

## Forgot your password?

Press **Forgot password?** on the start screen. Your admins and managers are told, and one of them resets it to `ChangeMe`; you then choose a new one. See [People, roles and passwords](/docs/people-and-passwords).

## Wrong room?

The room is picked once, at the start of the day, and the laptop stays in it: chat, the timer, captions and the overlay all follow the room. To change it, press **Sign out** and start again with the right room. A manager or admin can also move the laptop from **Laptops & screens** in the console.

## Main PC or Backup PC later

Press **This laptop** in the top bar to switch between Main PC and Backup PC, or **Test a pop-up** to see where pop-ups appear. See [Notifications, pop-ups and the bell](/docs/notifications).

## End of the day

Press **Sign out**, or just leave it: the laptop signs out by itself at **05:00** venue time, ready for the next show day. The laptop keeps its enrolment and its room, so tomorrow's tech only signs in.

## Admins and managers on a tech laptop

Signing in on the start screen with an admin or manager account opens the [manager view](/docs/manager-view) instead of a room. You don't need to pick a room. To run the console from a tech laptop's workspace, press **Admin** in its top bar and sign in: that session lasts 30 minutes and ends with the window.
