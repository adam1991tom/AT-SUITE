# Scenario: end of the show day

**The moment:** the last session in every room has finished.

## Each tech, in their room

1. **Timer**: **Stop**, so nothing is left counting or flashing on stage. Turn off **Blackout** and hide any message.
2. **Captions**: **Stop recording**, **Stop my mic**, **Clear the screens**.
3. **Transcripts**: if the client wants them, download **Text**, **SRT** or **VTT** now from **Caption settings → Transcripts**. They're deleted 12 hours after they end. See [Transcripts](/docs/transcripts).
4. **Handover notes**: anything tomorrow's crew needs: what broke, what's borrowed, what's charging. Tick off notes that are done. See [Handover notes](/docs/handover-notes).
5. **Help calls**: make sure your room's calls are **Resolved**, with how they were fixed.
6. **Sign out**. The laptop can stay on for tomorrow; the next tech picks the room in the morning.

## The duty manager

1. **Console → Help requests**: nothing left open.
2. **Show reports**: make the full venue report for today and **Download PDF** for the client, or **Keep a copy**. Each room's report too, if the client wants them. A show day runs to 05:00, so a report made after midnight still counts as today. See [Show reports](/docs/show-reports).
3. **Bug reports** (admins): anything new from the crew?
4. **Presenters**: set the event's **Status** to **finished** on the last day, or **Archive** it to switch off every presenter link.

## Overnight, by itself

- A full backup at 02:00 (see [Backups](/docs/backups)).
- The kept show reports for yesterday are made in the morning, for the venue and every room that had a show.
- Transcripts are deleted when their time is up.
- Each laptop's room for the day is cleared, so tomorrow's tech picks it fresh.
