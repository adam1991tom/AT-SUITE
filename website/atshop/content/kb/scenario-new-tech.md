# Scenario: bring a new tech on board

**You have:** a freelancer starting today in Studio 1. **Time:** 10 minutes.

## Before they arrive

1. **Console → People**: add them with a username (`sam.k`), their name (*Sam Khan*), role **tech** and their site. Leave the password blank: it's `ChangeMe`.
2. Leave a **handover note** in Studio 1 for them (see [Handover notes](/docs/handover-notes)): the session list, which mic is which, who to call.
3. Print the **tech card** from `/guide/tech` on your server, or send them the link. It's one page of the basics.

## When they sit down

1. On `STUDIO-1`, they type their username and `ChangeMe`, then choose their own password.
2. The room is already Studio 1. They choose **Main PC** and press **Start**. See [Start the day](/docs/start-the-day).

## The two-minute tour

Show them, in this order:

1. **The timer**: GO, Pause, +1m, and what the colours mean. See [Run the room timer](/docs/run-the-timer).
2. **Messages to the stage**: type, **Show**, **Hide**. See [Stage messages](/docs/stage-messages).
3. **Help** in the top bar: how to call for help, and the AI helper underneath it.
4. **Chat**: the room's channel and **All crew**. Mark something **Urgent** only if it is.
5. **Notes** in the top bar: the handover notes you left.
6. **Layout**: they can move windows around; **Reset to the starting layout** if they get lost.

## If they forget their password

They press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. You get an alert; reset them on **People** (or the manager view's **Password resets**) and they sign in with `ChangeMe` again. See [People, roles and passwords](/docs/people-and-passwords).

## At the end of their booking

On **People**, untick **Active** so their account stops working. Their name stays on show reports and in the audit log.
