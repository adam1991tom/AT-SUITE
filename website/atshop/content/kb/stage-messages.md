# Stage messages, blink, clock and blackout

Talk to the speaker without walking on stage.

## Show a quick message

Open **Messages** (a tab next to Quick timers) and tap a message: *Please wrap up*, *5 minutes*, *Time is up*, *Please come off stage* and more. It shows on the room's stage screens straight away, filling and gently pulsing on your button while it's up. Tap it again to take it off.

Tick **Blink** (top right of the window) to make it flash.

![The Messages window: buttons for Please wrap up, 5 minutes, 2 minutes, 1 minute, Time is up, Please come off stage and more, with a Blink tick box](/static/kb/workspace-messages.jpg)

![The Standard stage view showing the message 5 minutes in large white letters](/static/kb/screen-message.jpg "A stage screen showing a message.")

## Type your own

In the **Room timer** window, type in **Message to the stage** and press **Show**. **Hide** takes it off.

## Change the quick messages

Admins and managers can type a new quick message under the list in the workspace and save it for every room. In the console, **Timers → Quick messages** changes the whole list (one per line), or **Back to the defaults** puts it back.

![Timers, Quick messages tab in the console: the list of quick messages one per line, with Back to the defaults and Save messages](/static/kb/timers-messages.jpg)

## The second line

A line under the timer on the stage screens, like *Q&A next* or a countdown to something else. Open **Second line**:

- Type the text and press **Show text**.
- Or start a small countdown with **1m**, **2m**, **3m**, **5m**, **10m**, **15m**, **30m**, or type `mm:ss` and press **Set**, with optional text like *Q&A in*.

![The Second line window: a text box with Show text, and countdown buttons from 1m to 30m](/static/kb/workspace-second-line.jpg)

## Blink

**Blink** in the Room timer window flashes:

- a stage message that's showing,
- the timer, once time is up (at 0:00 and into overtime),
- the timer in the danger time.

Before time is up, Blink never flashes the timer. **Stop** clears it.

## Clock

**Clock** (in Quick timers or the Room timer) shows the time of day on the stage screens instead of the timer. Press it again for the timer.

## Blackout

**Blackout** blanks the room's stage screens. Press it again to bring them back. If a stage screen is black and you didn't mean it, check Blackout first.
