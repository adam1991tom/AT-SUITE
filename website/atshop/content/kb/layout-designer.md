# Make your own view in the layout designer

**Timers → Layout designer** (admins) makes timer views without any code: place the parts on a 16:9 screen, style them, and save. Every screen, workspace preview and laptop app showing the view updates straight away.

![The layout designer: parts to add on the left, the 16:9 canvas in the middle with a stage message, a big timer and Q&A in 5:00, and view settings on the right](/static/kb/timers-designer.jpg)

## Start a layout

1. Open **Timers → Layout designer**.
2. **Start from…** a ready-made layout: Standard, Speaker, Timer and clock, Lower third, or Blank.
3. Give it a **Name** under **View settings**, like *Client blue*.

## Add and place parts

**Add a part** has: Timer, Cue title, Next cue, Time of day, Stage message, Second line, Progress bar, Logo, Picture, Text, Room name, Status bar and Shape.

- **Drag** a part to move it; drag its corners to resize. Parts snap to the middle of the screen and to each other; hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while dragging to place freely.
- **With the keyboard**: arrow keys move the picked part (<kbd>Shift</kbd> for bigger steps), <kbd>Alt</kbd> and the arrows resize it, <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes it, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> copies it, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes.
- **Parts** lists what's on the screen; the top of the list is drawn on top. Use ▲ ▼ to change the order.

Each part has its own colour, background, round corners, text size, line up, bold and capitals.

## The view's look

Under **View settings**:

- **Background** and **text** colour, and the **font**.
- **Timer colours** for warning, danger and overtime, and **Flash a red border round the screen in overtime**.
- **See-through background**, for an overlay window or a vision mixer.
- **Pictures**: the view's **Logo** and **Background picture** (see below).

## Logos and pictures

![The layout designer with a client's logo top left, a dark blue background picture and a sponsor's logo in a Picture part top right; the Picture part is picked and its settings show Replace, Remove and Fit the picture](/static/kb/timers-designer-pictures.jpg)

Pictures can be PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP, up to 5 MB each. A PNG with a see-through background looks best for a logo. They're kept on the server with the view, so every screen showing it gets them.

- **The logo**: under **View settings → Pictures**, press **Upload…** next to **Logo**, or pick a **Logo** part on the screen and press **Upload…** in its settings. Every Logo part in the view shows it. It goes on the screens straight away.
- **A background picture**: **Upload…** next to **Background picture**. **Fit the picture** fills the screen, shows it all, or stretches it. It goes on the screens straight away.
- **More pictures** (a sponsor's logo, a photo, a show graphic): **Add a part → Picture** and choose the file, or drop picture files straight onto the screen. Each Picture part has its own picture; move and resize it like any part, and set **Fit the picture** to show it all, fill the box or stretch. Press **Save** to put it on the screens.
- **Replace…** swaps a picture; **Remove** takes it off.

A new layout is saved the moment you add its first picture, so give it a **Name** first. **Save as new** copies the pictures too.

## See it in every state

**Show it as** switches the canvas between Running, Warning, Danger, Overtime, Stage message and Clock, so you can check every state before the show.

## Save and use it

Press **Save** (or **Save as new** for a copy). The view appears in every screen's view list, the **Views** tab and the workspace's **Timer views ↗**. Views built with the older form open in the designer laid out the way they looked.
