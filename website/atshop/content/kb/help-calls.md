# Call for help, and answer help calls

When something goes wrong in a room, the tech presses **Help** and the crew sees it straight away, wherever they are.

## Call for help

1. Press **Help** in the top bar.
2. Pick what it's about: Audio, Video, Presenter, Lighting, Network or Other.
3. Say what's wrong, like "Projector says no signal from the lectern laptop".
4. Press **Send**.

![The Help menu open: Call for help with a category and a description, the open requests with one from Priya Shah in Studio 1, the AI helper box and Report a bug](/static/kb/workspace-help-menu.jpg)

The call goes to every console, the manager view, the backup PCs, and the backstage and speaker preview screens. One press sends one call: the button waits for the server, and the same call from the same person within 30 seconds counts as the one already sent.

While the crew is on the way, the [AI helper](/docs/ai-helper) opens with what you said and suggests what to try.

## Answer a help call

Help calls are cards with three buttons:

- **On my way**: tells the room someone's coming. The card says who.
- **Reply**: opens the room's chat, to ask a question.
- **Resolved**: closes it. It asks **How was it fixed?** inside the card. Fill it in: the AI helper offers that fix the next time someone has the same problem.

In the console, **Help requests** is a help desk: the calls on the left, the room's chat on the right, so you can answer straight away.

![The console's Help requests page: an open call from Studio 1 with On my way, Reply and Resolved, and the Studio 1 chat beside it](/static/kb/console-help.jpg)

## When nobody answers

A call nobody has answered with **On my way** goes out again after 2 minutes, then twice more at the same gap: to every laptop and every console and workspace page on the site. It's marked **no answer** in the help lists, and the backstage and speaker preview screens say **NO ANSWER** instead of HELP NEEDED. A Main PC still shows nothing, and there's never any sound.

An admin sets the minutes in **Settings → General** (0 turns it off).

## Where else help calls show

- The **Backstage** and **Speaker preview** screen views list them. See [Timer views](/docs/timer-views).
- The [show report](/docs/show-reports) lists every call, who answered and how long it took.
