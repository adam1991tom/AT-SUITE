# Show reports

A show report is a PDF of what happened in a room on a show day: every timer run and whether it ran over, help calls, stage messages, and captions. A **full venue** report puts every room together. Managers and admins make them on **Show reports** in the console.

![The Show reports page: a Make a report panel with Report for, Day, Include the crew's chat messages, a one-line summary, Download PDF and Keep a copy, and a list of kept reports below](/static/kb/console-reports.jpg "Console → Show reports.")

## Make one

1. **Report for**: the whole venue, or one room.
2. **Day**: a show day runs from 05:00 to 05:00 the next morning, so a late finish counts as the same day. Today's report shows what has happened so far.
3. Tick **Include the crew's chat messages** only if the client should see them.
4. Check the summary line: how many rooms had a show, timer runs, how many ran over and by how much in all, help calls (and how many had no answer at first), stage messages, chat messages and how long captions were live.
5. **Download PDF**.

**Keep a copy** saves the report on the server, in the list below, so you can download it again later.

## What's in it

- **Timer**: each run, with its start time, cue, session, planned length (and any time added), how long it really ran, and the result: **on time**, **2:10 to spare** or **over by 1:45** (in red).
- **Help calls**: the time, who asked, what for, who answered and how quickly, and how long until it was resolved. A call that had no answer at first says so.
- **Messages**: what was shown to the speaker on the stage screens, and how many crew chat messages were sent (how many urgent or important). The messages themselves only if you ticked **Include the crew's chat messages**: otherwise they stay private to the crew.
- **Captions**: when they were live and from which mic, and the transcript as captioned live, if one was recorded.

A full venue report starts with every room, then each room's sections in turn.

## Kept reports

Every morning, AT SUITE keeps a report by itself for the venue and for every room that had a show the day before (the timer ran, a help call, a stage message or captions). They're listed under **Kept reports** with **Download PDF** and **Delete**. Deleting one is fine: you can make it again while the day's records are still on the server.

> **Note:** Transcripts are deleted after 12 hours (see [Transcripts](/docs/transcripts)), so a report made later than that won't have the caption lines. The kept morning report is made before then.
