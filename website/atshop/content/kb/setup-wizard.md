# The setup wizard

The first time anyone opens a new AT SUITE server, it shows the setup wizard. It takes two minutes, and everything in it can be changed later in the console.

![The first page of the setup wizard with the organisation Grand Hall Events and the venue Riverside Centre filled in](/static/kb/setup-wizard.jpg)

## 1. Welcome

- **Organisation name**: your company, for example *Grand Hall Events*. It shows in the console and on show reports.
- **Venue (site) name**: the building, for example *Riverside Centre*.
- **Time zone**: where the venue is. The show day, the nightly backup and the morning reset all follow it.

## 2. Rooms

One room per line, for example *Main Hall*, *Studio 1*, *Studio 2*, *Boardroom*. Each room gets its own timer, chat, captions and laptops. You can add, rename and reorder rooms later under **Rooms**.

## 3. Administrator

The first account. It can do everything, including adding other admins. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and keep it safe. If you are the only admin, nobody else can reset it for you, so add a second admin under **People** once setup is done.

## 4. Licence

Paste the licence key from [your account](/account) (or from your supplier). With a valid key, AT SUITE works straight away. You can finish setup without one and paste it later in **Licence**, but nothing else works until you do. See [Licence keys](/docs/licence-keys).

Press **Finish setup** and you're signed in. AT SUITE opens the tech workspace for the first room; press **Admin** in its top bar (or open `#/dashboard`) for the console.
