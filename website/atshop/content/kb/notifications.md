# Notifications, pop-ups and the bell

AT SUITE never lets anything appear in front of the audience, and never makes a sound.

## Main PC

The laptop on the projector or stage feed. **Nothing pops up**, ever: no chat, no help calls, no notifications of any kind. Use it for the show.

## Backup PC

The laptop beside it. These pop up bottom-right, on top of everything, including full-screen slides:

- chat for your room,
- messages to all crew,
- urgent messages,
- help calls.

They never make a sound and never take the keyboard, so typing in another app isn't interrupted.

## Switch between them

Press **This laptop** in the top bar (in the AT SUITE app):

- **Main PC** or **Backup PC**.
- **Test a pop-up** shows one, so you know where they appear.
- **App version**, **Check for updates**, and **Update by itself when the app starts**. See [Updates](/docs/updates).

## The bell

![The bell open on a Backup PC: Notifications with All and Unread tabs, and two new handover notes](/static/kb/workspace-bell.jpg)

The bell in the top bar (on a Backup PC or in a browser) shows a red count of what's new: chat, help calls, handover notes and problems. Open it for **All** or **Unread**, sorted into **New** and **Earlier**, and **Mark all as read**.

Timer warnings still pop up when time is nearly up, but never go in the count.

## In the windows

- The **Chat** window glows and the new message lights up for a few seconds.
- Windows and the **Layout** menu show a count for anything unread.
