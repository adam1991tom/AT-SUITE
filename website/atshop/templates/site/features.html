Show timers

  • Room timers with GO, pause, next and previous, plus or minus a minute, blink and blackout
  • The colour changes as time runs out: amber with five minutes left, red for the last minute, then it counts up in overtime with a flashing border
  • A cue list with times, notes, overtime rules, and import from Ontime
  • One-tap quick timers, and quick messages to the stage that admins can edit
  • A second line under the timer for a second countdown or a short note
  • The time of day between sessions, and a blackout when the screen should go dark
  • The timer keeps running on the laptop if the network drops

Stage screens, your way

  • Views for stage, minimal, clock, backstage and speaker preview, plus a transparent overlay for OBS or vMix
  • A layout designer: place the timer, title, clock, message, progress bar, logos and sponsors' pictures on a 16:9 screen
  • Pair any screen by typing the six-digit code it shows, then choose what it shows from the workspace
  • Turn a Linux laptop or all-in-one into a stage screen with one command, with remote restart and update
  • Test patterns for setting up: colour bars, gradients, LED tile map, sharpness and overscan
  • The timer over a laptop's own screen with AT Overlay, and control of AT LiveOverlay graphics laptops

Live captions, kept private

  • Speech recognition runs on the tech's laptop, offline, from its microphone. Audio and text never go to the cloud
  • A vocabulary list for names and jargon, plus live corrections while it runs
  • A full-screen audience caption screen, a subtitle bar, and a transparent overlay for streams
  • Mic gain and EQ, fonts, sizes and colours set per room, with a live preview
  • Transcripts are encrypted, export as text, SRT or VTT, and are deleted after 12 hours by default
  • The laptop can feed the room's caption screens itself, so they keep going if the server can't be reached

Captions are automatic and can be wrong. For a deaf attendee who relies on them, book a professional captioner as well.

Crew chat, help and handovers

  • Messenger-style chat for all crew and for each room, with files, emoji, priority messages, and "Seen by"
  • A Help button that calls the right people, then goes out to the whole crew if nobody answers
  • A help desk in the console: on my way, reply, resolved
  • Handover notes so the next shift knows what happened, with pinned notes for the whole run
  • A bell that counts what's new, while the Main PC stays clean for the show
  • Links to Companion buttons, camera control or a floor plan, on the dashboard or in a room

An AI helper for show-day faults

  • Say what's wrong and keep talking until it's fixed: it asks questions to narrow the fault down
  • It learns from how your crew fixed things before, and from your venue's own notes
  • It answers "how do I…" questions from the AT SUITE manual, step by step
  • One tap turns the chat into a help call when it needs a person
  • Admins choose where it runs, including privately on your own server

Presenters and their slides

  • Events with sessions and presenters for each room
  • A private page for each presenter to upload slides and check in, with no account needed
  • Review and approve files before they reach the room
  • Room sync keeps the presentation laptop's folder in step with every approved deck

Every laptop in view

  • The Windows tech app: each tech's own workspace, with windows they can arrange, dock and pop out
  • Techs sign in and pick their room; Main PC and Backup PC modes keep the show screen clean
  • Laptop health and what each one is showing (off by default; never saved)
  • Roll the app out silently, and it updates itself from your server
  • The workspace opens even if the server can't be reached

Running the venue

  • A venue dashboard, and a manager view with every room's timer, help calls and chat in one place
  • A pre-show check that shows which laptops, screens and caption mics are ready
  • Show reports as PDF, for one room or the whole venue side by side
  • The venue's weather on the dashboard, for load-ins and outdoor shows
  • Rooms, sites and room locks, with roles for admins, managers and techs
  • Nightly backups of the whole server, and a move to new hardware is a download and an upload
  • An audit log of who did what, exportable as PDF

And everything around it

🔒Secure by design

Tech passwords set by admins, with a forced change at first sign-in. No passwords or keys in web addresses. Captions stay on the laptop, for NDA and GDPR peace of mind.

⚙Automation

A Bitfocus Companion module and a REST API with API keys: timers, messages, overlays, captions and screens from a button.

☁Your server or ours

Run AT SUITE on your own server, or let us host it and just install the laptop app. Updates install themselves, never during a show.

?Help built in

A full manual inside the app that works with no internet, the same pages on our help centre, and step-by-step show-day scenarios.

🔑Licences that never stop a show

Keys renew themselves. An expired licence gets 14 days of grace, and any lock waits until no show is on.

🐞Report a bug

Report a bug from any laptop or the console, with what you were doing and a screenshot. Your admins see every report and can pass it on to us.
